Privacy Policy

Kobalt Construction ("we," "us," or "our") respects your privacy. This policy explains what information we collect through this website and how we use it.

Information we collect. When you submit our Contact form or a job application, we collect the information you provide directly, which may include your name, email address, phone number, company name, and message content. For job applications, this may also include your mailing address, driver's license information, work history, and references. This applies to applications submitted through this website and to our printable application form if you send it to us.

How we use it. We use this information solely to respond to your inquiry or to evaluate your job application. We do not sell or rent your information to third parties.

Analytics. We use Google Analytics to understand how visitors use this site, such as which pages are viewed and what kind of device and browser are used. Google Analytics uses cookies and similar technologies and may receive information such as your approximate location and activity on this site. We review this information in aggregate to improve the site. To learn more about how Google handles this data, see policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites, or opt out with Google's browser add-on at tools.google.com/dlpage/gaoptout.

Other third-party services. To display this site, your browser loads fonts from Google Fonts and code libraries from Cloudflare's cdnjs, and, on pages with our project map, map images from MapTiler using OpenStreetMap data. These providers receive your IP address and basic browser information as a normal part of delivering those files.

Third-party form processing. Form submissions on this site are processed by Formspree, a third-party form-handling service, which delivers submissions to our email inbox. Formspree's own privacy practices govern how they handle data in transit; you can review their policy at formspree.io.

External links. This site links to third-party organizations (industry associations, government agencies, social media, mapping services, and similar). Those sites have their own privacy practices, which we don't control.

Children. This site is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them.

Changes to this policy. This policy was last updated in October 2026. We may update it from time to time. Questions about this policy can be directed to info@kobaltconstruction.com.

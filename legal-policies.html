Privacy Policy

Kobalt Construction ("we," "us," or "our") respects your privacy. This policy explains what information we collect through this website and how we use it.

Information we collect. When you submit our Contact form or a job application, we collect the information you provide directly — which may include your name, email address, phone number, company name, message content, and, if you choose to include one, a resume file.

How we use it. We use this information solely to respond to your inquiry or to evaluate your job application. We do not sell or rent your information to third parties.

Third-party form processing. Form submissions on this site are processed by Formspree, a third-party form-handling service, which delivers submissions to our email inbox. Formspree's own privacy practices govern how they handle data in transit; you can review their policy at formspree.io.

External links. This site links to third-party organizations (industry associations, government agencies, and similar). Those sites have their own privacy practices, which we don't control.

Changes to this policy. We may update this policy from time to time. Questions about this policy can be directed to info@kobaltconstruction.com.

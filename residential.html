What We Build

Residential Services

New home construction and additions, built to the same standard we bring to public infrastructure.

Whether it's a new build or an addition, we bring quality materials, licensed and bonded crews, and a timeline you can plan around to every residential project.

Our residential work today centers on Hilltop Heights, a new residential development taking shape in Scranton, where our masonry and site crews are laying the foundation for the community's homes from the ground up.

What This Includes

New Home Construction

Ground-up residential builds, from foundation to finished frame.

Home Additions & Renovations

Expanding or updating existing homes with the same quality standard as new builds.

Site Work, Excavation & Foundations

Grading, excavation, and block or poured foundations built to engineering spec.

Driveways & Concrete Flatwork

Durable concrete surfaces built to handle daily use and Pennsylvania winters.

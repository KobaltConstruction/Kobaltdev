Who We Are

Built By The Poconos, For The Poconos

Kobalt Construction is a full-service general contractor based in Tobyhanna, Pennsylvania. For more than 15 years, we've partnered with commercial, residential, and public clients across the Pocono Mountains, Lehigh Valley, Wyoming Valley, and Greater Scranton to build projects that last.

Nestled in the picturesque Pocono Mountains, our central location allows us to efficiently serve a broad spectrum of areas — spanning from the bustling urban centers of the Greater Lehigh Valley, including Allentown and Bethlehem, to the thriving communities of the Wyoming Valley, Scranton, and beyond.

Proudly affiliated with the Pocono Builders Association, our team comprises seasoned professionals with years of hands-on experience in construction and site work. Every member of our crew has completed Construction Safety and Health training certified by the U.S. Department of Labor's OSHA, and we're fully bonded and licensed for your peace of mind.

Equipment Operator

As an Equipment Operator, you'll run the heavy machinery behind every Kobalt project — from excavation and grading to final site prep.

Responsibilities

  • Operate excavators, bulldozers, loaders, and other heavy equipment for grading and excavation
  • Read grade stakes, site plans, and drawings to execute work accurately
  • Perform routine equipment inspections and basic maintenance checks
  • Work closely with foremen and crew members to keep projects on schedule
  • Follow all safety protocols around equipment operation and jobsite traffic

Requirements

  • Prior experience operating heavy construction equipment
  • Ability to read and interpret grade stakes and site plans
  • Strong safety mindset and attention to surroundings
  • Valid driver's license; CDL a plus

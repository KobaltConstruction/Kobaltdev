Public Project — Dallas, PA

Dallas Memorial Park Improvements

Dallas Memorial Park has been getting a full slate of recreational upgrades, and our site team — led by Chris and Mike — has handled the work for the Borough of Dallas and project engineer Quad 3 Engineers.

The scope covers new basketball and pickleball courts, new parking lots, sports surfacing, sidewalks, and fencing throughout the park. As the project nears completion, our paving team, led by Adrien, has been on-site wrapping up trail paving and the new ADA-accessible parking lot.

With courts, parking, and pathways all part of the same coordinated scope, the improvements give the Dallas community a refreshed, more accessible park to enjoy for years to come.

Project Team & Partners

Client: Borough of Dallas. Engineer: Quad 3 Engineers.

Paving & Site Work in Hazleton and Mountain Top

School and borough paving, a park upgrade, and hillside stabilization around Hazleton, Mountain Top, and Carbon County.

Around Hazleton and Mountain Top, we've resurfaced a school district parking lot and a West Hazleton Borough street, upgraded 12th Ward Park with new play equipment and a fenced sports court, and built a retaining wall and paved roadway at Crestwood School. Farther south in Carbon County, our crews cleared and developed the 10-acre Carbon County Emergency Training Center site in Nesquehoning.

These are the projects we've documented around Hazleton and Mountain Top, with a summary of the work our crews did on each.

Across these 5 projects, our crews handled site work on 3 and paving on 4.
